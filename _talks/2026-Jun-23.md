In this talk, we provide a full presentation of a suite of Hybrid Model calculations of EECs for jets with varying tranverse momenta and radii in OO collisions. Since the energy lost by jets due to strongly coupled interactions with the medium is smaller in OO collisions than in PbPb collisions, we show that EECs suffer far less from jet-energy selection biases in OO collisions. Although jets in OO collisions lose less energy than jets in PbPb collisions, jet-induced wakes and Molière scatterings modify the structure of jets in OO collisions enough to significantly enhance EECs at large angular separations between jet-constituents. We show that increasing the jet-radius allows one to amplify the effects of jet-induced wakes. The wake effects can be suppressed by calculating EECs using only hadrons with tranverse momenta greater than 2 GeV, allowing the effects of Molière scatterings to dominate and making them easy to isolate, since hadrons originating from jet-induced wakes are soft. One on hand, imposing the cut on $R=0.4$ jets, we show that Molière scattering leads to large angle correlations that enhance the OO/pp ratio of EECs. On the other hand, imposing the same cut with $R = 0.8$ jets reveals Molière scattering as a bump in the OO/pp ratio of EECs. Furthermore, the bump location shifts towards smaller angle as the jet momentum is increased. A systematic study of the bump location in a simple model, as well as how the bump location depends on QGP properties and model parameters is shown, where we suggest a possible scaling relationship between bump position, jet momentum, and QGP temperature. We thus suggest that evidence of Molière scattering in QGP can be found by measuring EECs in OO collisions, and that quantifying the bump in the EEC ratio can be a big step towards understanding the microscpoic structure of QGP.
